Tab-Quest: The Dungeon Crawler

your tabs are monsters… deal with it

Tab-Quest is a chrome extension that turns your browsing into a small RPG. every tab becomes a monster and your hero auto-fights them from the side panel. kill monsters than tabs close →and than browser becomes clean

basically… productivity but in a weird gamer way
